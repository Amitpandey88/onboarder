PRIVACY / PLAIN LANGUAGE

Privacy.

This is a static portfolio. There are no accounts, contact forms, analytics scripts or advertising trackers on this site.

Last updated 23 November 2026

What this site stores

The only browser storage written by this site is amit-theme in local storage. It remembers whether you chose the light or dark appearance. It is not a visitor identifier and can be removed through your browser’s site-data controls.

Delivery and security

This site is delivered through Cloudflare and an Nginx web server. Those services may process request metadata such as IP address, user agent, requested URL and time for delivery, abuse prevention and security. Cloudflare may apply its own security cookies or challenge behavior depending on the request. Those services have their own privacy terms.

Email and external links

If you choose an email link, your mail provider and the receiving mail service process the message. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, npm, theamit.me and Onboarder leave this site; those services apply their own privacy and cookie practices.

No profiling

This site does not build a profile of you, sell visitor data, or use third-party advertising or behavioural analytics.

Questions

For a privacy question, email hello@theamit.me. I may update this page if the site’s data handling changes; the date above records the latest revision.
