1. Who we are and what this covers

Intellimatrix is an independent software project created and operated by Amit Pandey, based in India. This policy covers the public Intellimatrix and Onboarder websites at intellimatrix.in and onboarder.intellimatrix.in.

Separate applications, including the Q3 research workspace, and external services may handle information differently. This policy describes these two marketing websites and explains how the separately installed Onboarder tool differs.

2. Information we handle

  • Website requests: the hosting server and Cloudflare handle technical information such as IP address, requested URL, request time, browser user agent, response status, and referring page where supplied.
  • Your theme choice: choosing light or dark mode saves a preference cookie and browser storage. See the Cookies page for details and a reset control.
  • Messages you send: if you email us, we receive your address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to include.
  • Interactive example: file selections in the example map are processed in browser memory. This website does not scan or upload your repository.
  • Public contributions: GitHub issues, comments, and pull requests are handled by GitHub and may be visible publicly.

These websites have no accounts, repository upload service, or payment checkout. The example repository map does not read files from your device. Fonts, images, and videos are served from the website.

3. Why we use this information

We use technical information to deliver pages, investigate errors, maintain reliability, and protect the sites against abuse. Theme storage remembers a preference you select. Information in your messages is used to answer questions, provide support, investigate reports, and follow up on the conversation.

We do not sell personal information or use these websites to build advertising profiles.

4. Service providers and external links

Our hosting infrastructure delivers the sites. Cloudflare sits in front of the server for delivery and security, and handles request information and traffic statistics. Depending on enabled security features, it may set security cookies. Its processing can occur in countries outside your own; see Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy and cookie documentation.

Email providers process messages when you contact us. Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, theamit.me, and Buy Me a Coffee take you to external websites with their own terms and privacy practices. Payments or support contributions happen on Buy Me a Coffee; these sites do not receive your card details.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where necessary to investigate abuse and protect lawful rights.

5. Onboarder software and optional AI

The installable Onboarder tool analyzes repositories locally. Loading a Git URL contacts the Git host. Optional AI workflows send task context and requested source to the model provider you configure. Local conversations and workspaces can remain on your device so work can resume.

Your chosen provider’s terms, credentials, and data policies apply to AI requests. Review what you send and avoid including secrets or confidential material without authorization. This marketing page does not receive your local repository or AI conversations. See the Onboarder documentation for current software behavior.

6. Retention and security

Origin server logs are configured for daily rotation with up to 14 rotated log files. This describes the server’s rotation setting, not a guaranteed deletion deadline for Cloudflare or other providers, whose retention follows their own settings and policies.

Messages are kept as needed for the conversation, support history, and any applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of information you sent; we will explain any information that needs to be retained.

We use HTTPS and restrict server access. No internet transmission or storage method can guarantee absolute security. Please report a suspected issue through the private email link on the Contact page.

7. Your choices and requests

You can clear cookies and browser storage, reset your theme preference, browse without contacting us, and choose whether to use external links or optional AI services.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, objection, or other handling of your personal information. Email hello@theamit.me with your request and enough context to identify the relevant information. We may need to verify that the request relates to you before responding. Please do not send passwords, API keys, or unnecessary identity documents.

8. Changes and contact

We will update this page when these practices change and show the revised date above. For privacy questions or concerns, contact Amit Pandey at hello@theamit.me.
