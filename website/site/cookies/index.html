Cookies & preferences.

What we save, why it is saved, and how to reset your display preference.

Effective and last updated: 6 October 2026

1. Your theme preference

When you choose light or dark mode, we save the value light or dark in two places. This stores a display choice, not a unique visitor identifier.

COOKIE

im-theme

Shared across intellimatrix.in and its subdomains. Set for up to one year from your latest theme selection, with Secure on HTTPS and SameSite=Lax.

LOCAL STORAGE

im-theme

Saved separately for each website origin as a fallback. Browser storage has no built-in expiry; it remains until you clear it or use the reset below.

If no preference has been saved, the site follows your device’s light/dark setting when the page loads. We do not add advertising cookies or a marketing tracker in the website code.

2. Cloudflare security and traffic handling

Cloudflare delivers and protects these websites and handles request metadata and traffic statistics. Depending on the protection applied to a request, it may set cookies for bot protection or a security challenge. The precise cookies depend on Cloudflare’s features; see its cookie reference and Privacy Policy.

Links to external services use those services’ own cookie settings when you visit them.

3. Manage your preferences

Reset removes the shared theme cookie and the theme value stored for this website, then follows your device’s setting. To clear the local fallback on the other website too, use its Cookies page or your browser’s site-data controls. This control does not remove security cookies or data stored by external providers.

CURRENT APPEARANCEDevice default

You can also block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser. Pages remain readable, but the theme may no longer persist and Cloudflare security checks may be affected.

4. Find out more

Read our Privacy Policy for request logs, contact messages, and your information choices. For questions, contact Amit.
