INDEPENDENT BY DESIGN

A compass for curious developers.

Onboarder helps developers find a path through unfamiliar code. It brings architecture, imports, and local reviews into a workspace you can explore before you change anything.

Open source is part of the process: share the work, learn from feedback, and keep improving. Onboarder is in public beta. Q3 is an experimental research workspace. More projects are in the making.

Make complexity visible

02 / BUILD IN THE OPEN

03 / KNOW YOUR TOOLS

Keep control
